===================
Branding guidelines
===================

This page describes what we see as appropriate usage of the ccAPPS name, logo and branding.

* | The official name is **ccAPPS**, with PPL capitalized. Alternatively you can use
    the name in lower case or upper case.
  | This name is also legally registered as a
    `trade mark <https://euipo.europa.eu/eSearch/#details/trademarks/018891700>`_.

* The official tag line is **the world's most advanced open source supply chain
  planning solution**.

* The official logo is available in SVG format:

  .. image:: ccAPPS.svg
      :height: 50px

* We consider the following as approved usage of the ccAPPS name. No approval
  is needed for any of the following.

  * You can use our software in your own software or documentation, which is based on or
    extends ccAPPS. The derived products can't be called "ccAPPS", but calling them
    "powered by ccAPPS" or "derived from ccAPPS" is correct.

  * You can use the ccAPPS name and logo in any publication or public event.

  * You may call yourself a corporate or individual contributor, provided you have
    authored at least 1 pull request that was successfully merged in the code.

  * On any online presence we highly appreciate links to https://ccAPPS.com.

* We consider inappropriate usage of the ccAPPS name:

  * any use that is completely unrelated to ccAPPS, does not run on it or work with it

  * to suggest that your usage is sponsored by, affiliated with, endorsed by or approved
    by the ccAPPS company when this isn't the case.

* When in doubt whether your usage is correct, please contact us at info@ccAPPS.com.
  We're nice people and will gladly review your specific situation.
